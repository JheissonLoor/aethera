Aethera has been completely transformed from a solid long-distance relationship app into an **ultra-modern, emotionally resonant, and deeply innovative** platform. This document provides a visual overview of everything that was added.

## 6 Revolutionary Features

### 1. Pulse Moments
**Send heartbeats of emotion to your partner in real-time**

```
You                          Partner
[Select Emotion] ──────────────> [Receive with Animation]
[Type Message]   Sync Status   [See Your Message]
[Send] ──────────────────────> [Animated Pulse Effect]
         ────────────────────> [Tap to Acknowledge]
[See Acknowledged] <────────────────
```

**Visual Experience:**
- Pulsing colored orb that animates heartbeat-style
- Emotion colors (love=pink, joy=gold, peace=teal, etc.)
- Haptic feedback on send
- Real-time sync across devices
- Acknowledgment confirmation

## Advanced UI/UX Components

        └─ Shows all navigation options

**Features:**
- Smooth morphing animations
